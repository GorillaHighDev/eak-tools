When something looks wrong

  • "Complete the fit" is empty on product pages — the nightly rebuild didn't run. Check the Actions tab before assuming the engine broke.
  • A sold-out product is still in a grid — collection rules carry inventory > 0; a handful of OR-rule collections hide sold-out cards in the theme instead. The product stays live either way.
  • A storefront change doesn't show — pages are cached for minutes, and a gated store still returns 200. Re-check with a cache-buster before changing anything else.
  • The phone tool looks out of date — it's a saved web app: force-refresh it after an update.
  • A marketing text won't send — editing an SMS puts it back to Draft; turn it on again. A second marketing text to the same person within a few hours is refused.
  • Four emails suddenly paused — editing the TAPIN15 discount pauses every automation that references it. Check Messaging → Automations for the red icon.

Rollbacks

  • The site looks wrong after a theme change — publish the previous theme from Online Store → Themes. Each big change leaves a dated backup theme.
  • The cloud jobs stop — the Windows tasks still exist on the shop PC, disabled. Re-enable them and turn the workflows off, never both at once.
